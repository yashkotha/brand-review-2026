zippyy.ai|Captured 5 Oct 2026|Homepage, 20 inner pages, every blog post, competitor Instagram feeds|Desktop 1440px and iPhone 390px

Zippyy sells AI logistics software to Indian D2C brands and logistics teams. It was GoDash until recently. The core identity is strong and usable: coral on charcoal, a distinctive Z mark, real product screens. The problem is everything around it. Leftover text from other companies, two different business stories, off-brand graphics and unbacked numbers. Below: the audit, a brand strategy, and 12 ready social posts.

  • Charcoal and paper alternate, so the feed reads as a checkerboard from a distance
